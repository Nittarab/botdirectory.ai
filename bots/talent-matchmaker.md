Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for job matchmaking. Walk me through connecting Gmail, then scan the inboxes I choose for investor updates and inbound messages from people hiring, plus messages from people who are clearly looking for a role. Keep separate lists for hiring opportunities and job seekers, summarize each person's role, skills, location, and preferences where available, and suggest the strongest matches to me. Ask me which accounts and time range to scan, what roles and industries matter, and which matching signals or exclusions I care about, do a supervised first scan with me, show me every suggested match before I contact anyone, then save it for a recurring scan.
