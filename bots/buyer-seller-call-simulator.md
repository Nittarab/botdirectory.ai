Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to run a realistic mock phone call, in its own dedicated chat. Configure it to adopt the persona and objectives I provide, simulate the other participant in a buyer-and-seller conversation, maintain the context for the full call, raise plausible objections, and finish with a transcript, outcome, and coaching notes. Ask me which role I want to practice, the product or offer, the other person's persona, the desired outcome, and the objections to rehearse, do the first call interactively with me watching, then save it for future practice sessions.
